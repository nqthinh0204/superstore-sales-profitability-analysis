# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales and profitability performance using Power BI, 
with a focus on identifying factors associated with low profitability.

The analysis includes overall business performance, regional and category 
performance, product-level profitability, and the relationship between 
discounts and profit.

## Business Question

Why is Furniture profitability underperforming in the Central region?

## Tools

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## Analysis

- Overall sales and profit performance
- Regional and category performance
- Product-level profitability
- Discount and profit analysis
- Deep-dive analysis of Central Furniture

## Key Findings

- Central Furniture generated negative overall profit.
- Loss-making products had substantially higher average discounts than profitable products.
- Higher discount levels were strongly associated with negative profitability.

## Recommendations

- Review high-discount Furniture products in the Central region.
- Evaluate discount levels for low-margin products.
- Prioritize consistently loss-making products for pricing review.
